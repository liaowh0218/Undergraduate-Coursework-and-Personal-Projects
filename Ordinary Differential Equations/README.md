# EECS203001 Ordinary Differential Equations
Offered by [Interdiciplinary Program of Electircal Engineering and Computer Science](https://u-eecs.site.nthu.edu.tw/)

**Grade:** In Progress (Spring 2026)

**Textbook:** [Advanced Engineering Mathematics](https://www.amazon.com/Advanced-Engineering-Mathematics-Erwin-Kreyszig/dp/0470458364) CH 1-6 and CH 11

## About
This is a bonus project in ODE. It is a problem about dynamical variation in temperature of tub(T) water which is affected by room temperature(T_room), temperature of drained in water(T_in), initial water temperature in the tub(T_0), flow rate of the water(R) and heat loss coefficient(k).
