Offered by [Department of Quantitative Finance](https://qf.site.nthu.edu.tw/)

**Grade:** A+ (Spring 2025)

**Programming Language:** Python

**Material:** Provided by Instructor

## About
This is the final group project in Introduction to Programming.

We selected 15 stocks from S&P 500, and compare the strategy between **technical analysis** and **buy and hold**. I was responsible for the buy and hold part. I got the historical data from yfinance and constructed the efficient frontier by Monte-Carlo simulation. Then took the tangent line through the origin to determine the weights.
